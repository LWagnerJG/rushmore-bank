# How to Play — Beans

1. **Create / Join** — nickname + room code, link, or QR. Host starts at 2–10 players.
2. **Topic** — vote the spun shortlist (or host custom).
3. **Draft** — snake draft four answers right away. Shared board fills like a fantasy draft. **Lock In** on your turn. Private “My Ideas” stay on your phone.
4. **Review / Vote** — skim rosters; vote for someone else’s.
5. **Scores** — beans = 20 + AI award + 5× votes.
6. **Wager** — slider for how many beans to risk (protected vs at-risk).
7. **Bank** — each player gets their own bank turn: two safe rolls, then risk. **Bank** locks the pot. Waiting players can Bank early.
8. **Next topic** or end — most banked beans wins.

Optional **Party Mode** (off by default): light sip prompts after busts or wins. Pass anytime — no score effect.
